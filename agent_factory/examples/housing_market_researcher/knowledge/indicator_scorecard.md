# Housing Indicator Scorecard

Use a repeatable scorecard so every market write-up covers the same core housing signals.

Supply:
- Active listings year over year
- Months of supply
- Days on market
- New listings trend

Demand:
- Pending sales year over year
- Closed sales year over year
- Migration trend
- Builder traffic and backlog comments

Affordability and macro:
- Mortgage rate level
- Mortgage rate change in basis points
- Employment growth
- Unemployment rate
- Insurance and tax pressure when material

Pricing and concessions:
- Median sale price year over year
- List to sale ratio
- Share of listings with price reductions
- Seller or builder concession rate

Pipeline and competitive pressure:
- Permits and starts trend
- Completion pipeline versus absorption
- Rent growth and vacancy for investor-sensitive markets

Interpretation rules:
- Tight inventory with improving pending sales usually points toward a heating market.
- Rising inventory, slower pending sales, and larger concessions usually point toward a cooling market.
- Mixed signals across supply and demand usually point toward a balanced market that needs segment-level analysis.
