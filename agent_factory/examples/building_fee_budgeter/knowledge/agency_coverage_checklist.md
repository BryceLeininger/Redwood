# Agency Coverage Checklist

The budget is incomplete until the responsible agencies are identified for the actual parcel and project scope.

Typical agencies to verify:

- city or county building department
- public works or development services department for impact fees
- water district
- sewer or sanitation district
- fire district or fire facilities authority
- school district
- special districts that collect drainage, roadway, habitat, or community facilities charges

Parcel and project facts that drive fee selection:

- city vs county jurisdiction
- annexation status
- land use and housing type
- dwelling unit count or lot count
- residential or commercial square footage
- building valuation basis
- service connection count and meter size
- phased permit timing

If any of these items are unknown, the budget should be labeled as incomplete until the missing agency or quantity is confirmed.
