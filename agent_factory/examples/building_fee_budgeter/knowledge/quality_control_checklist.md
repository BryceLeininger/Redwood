# Quality Control Checklist

Before issuing a building and impact fee budget:

- Verify every line item points to an official schedule, ordinance, or adopted resolution.
- Confirm the fee schedule effective date is current as of the pricing date.
- Match the parcel to the correct city, county, utility, and school district service areas.
- Confirm whether plan check, inspection, technology, and records surcharges are separate from the permit fee.
- Check whether any fee is per unit, per square foot, per valuation, per meter, or phased by permit timing.
- Note any exclusions such as reimbursement agreements, oversized utility frontage, negotiated mitigation, or deferred school fee timing.
- Recalculate all formulas after any change to unit count, square footage, valuation, or service size.

The agent should escalate missing schedule versions, missing effective dates, or unresolved agency coverage instead of filling gaps with old assumptions.
