# Official Fee Budgeting Playbook

Building and impact fee budgets should only be presented as exact when they are traced to current adopted fee schedules from every applicable agency.

Core workflow:

1. Confirm the parcel jurisdiction and every collecting agency before pricing anything.
2. Gather the current official fee schedules, resolutions, and utility charge tables.
3. Record the effective date and source link or document citation for every agency.
4. Normalize each fee into an explicit formula tied to project quantities such as valuation, units, square footage, meter count, or service size.
5. Refuse to guess when district boundaries, valuation basis, meter size, or school fee applicability are unresolved.
6. Reconcile the line-item math against the source schedule before sharing totals.

Common categories to capture:

- building permit
- plan check
- inspections and technology surcharges
- traffic, park, public facilities, and fire impact fees
- water and sewer connection or capacity charges
- school district statutory or negotiated fees

A reliable budget packet shows the formula used for each line item, the governing source, the effective date, and any exclusions such as reimbursement agreements or deferred timing.
