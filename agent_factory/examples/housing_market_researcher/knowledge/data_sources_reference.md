# Housing Market Data Source Reference

Reliable housing research usually blends multiple source types rather than relying on a single dashboard.

Public and low-friction sources:
- FRED for mortgage rates, employment, unemployment, permits, starts, completions, house price indexes, and rent-related series.
- U.S. Census for building permits, starts, completions, and population context.
- FHFA and Freddie Mac for home price and mortgage market context.
- Local MLS or brokerage market reports for listings, pending sales, days on market, concessions, and list-to-sale ratios.
- Builder surveys, earnings calls, and local permit portals for forward-looking supply signals.

Research process:
1. Start with rate, labor, and supply trend checks.
2. Add local resale data for pricing, velocity, and concessions.
3. Compare completion pipeline against demand momentum.
4. Separate entry-level, move-up, luxury, and rental signals before reaching a conclusion.

Common reporting mistake:
- Calling a market hot because prices are still up year over year even when inventory, concessions, and pending sales are already deteriorating.
